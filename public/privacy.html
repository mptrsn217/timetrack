Privacy

Moonglare stores only what it needs to work:

  • From your Google account: your email address, name and profile picture URL, used to identify your account.
  • The activities you create, the start and stop times you record, any notes you add to entries, and your goals.
  • If you turn on notifications: an address from your browser's push service (Apple, Google or Mozilla) so we can send them, your notification settings, and your time zone so reminders follow your local day. Notifications contain only activity names and times.

Your data is visible only to you. It is not sold, shared or used for advertising. Sign-in uses a single cookie that keeps you logged in; there are no analytics or tracking cookies.

You can export all your data as CSV or download a full backup at any time, and "Delete account" at the bottom of the app permanently removes your account and everything you tracked.
